# Starship-Shooter

A mini starship shooting game where the player must survive waves of enemies and hazards until the end. Pilot your ship, dodge incoming attacks, and shoot your way through increasingly challenging levels to reach victory.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Control a **starship** and fight against enemies/obstacles
- **Shoot** to destroy threats and clear the path
- **Survive until the end** to win the game
- Difficulty increases over time (faster enemies, more projectiles, tougher patterns)
